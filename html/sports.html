USA vs. Mexico: 2025 Concacaf Gold Cup

By: Jose Bautista, Jul 6, 2025

Mexico beat the USA 2-1 to win their 10th CONCACAF Gold Cup title on July 6, 2025, at NRG Stadium in Houston, Texas. The US took an early lead with a Chris Richards header, but Mexico's Raúl Jiménez equalized before halftime, and Edson Álvarez scored the winning goal in the 77th minute, a goal that was initially ruled offside but was awarded after a VAR review. What a game!
